Odliadur

Welsh end-rhyme finder. Type an ending to see the words that share it.

Welsh has two different wy sounds that do not rhyme with each other (lleddf, as in mwyn, and talgron, as in gwyn). This version lists every word that ends in the letters you typed, so it does not separate them yet.

    Word list: the Welsh spelling dictionary from Bangor University's Language Technologies Unit (Uned Technolegau Iaith) and Troi, licensed under the GNU LGPL v3 or later. Source: techiaith/hunspell-cy. Plural forms were added by adding the regular endings that dictionary defines.

    Meanings: fetched live from Wiktionary, available under CC BY-SA. Many rare words have no entry there; for those, try Geiriadur Prifysgol Cymru (link under each word).

    This is a personal tool inspired by the book Yr Odliadur. It contains no text from that book, and it is not affiliated with its authors or publisher. A list of words that share an ending is not the same as a list of words that rhyme in strict-metre poetry, so check against a printed odliadur for important work.
