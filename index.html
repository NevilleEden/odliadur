Odliadur

Welsh end-rhyme finder. Type an ending to see the words that share it.

One-syllable endings in alphabetical order. Use the arrows to move along; searching centres the list on your ending.

Welsh has two different wy sounds that do not rhyme with each other (lleddf, as in mwyn, and talgron, as in gwyn). This version lists every word that ends in the letters you typed, so it does not separate them yet.

    Word list: the Welsh spelling dictionary from Bangor University's Language Technologies Unit (Uned Technolegau Iaith) and Troi, licensed under the GNU LGPL v3 or later. Source: techiaith/hunspell-cy. Plural forms were added by adding the regular endings that dictionary defines.

    Meanings: most come from Wiktionary (Welsh entries, extracted with kaikki.org), available under CC BY-SA 4.0. About 4,000 more come from the Welsh–English data of the Apertium project (based on Eurfa by Kevin Donnelly and others), under the GNU GPL v2. Roughly a quarter of the words have a meaning; for the rest, tap the word and try the Wiktionary or Geiriadur Prifysgol Cymru links. Tapping a word can also fetch the fuller Wiktionary entry live.

    Inspired by the book Yr Odliadur by Roy Stephens and Alan Llwyd (ISBN 978-1-84323-903-1). This is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors or publisher. It does not reproduce the book, and its word list differs from the book's. A list of words that share an ending is not the same as a list of words that rhyme in strict-metre poetry, so for the authoritative guide to rhyme, including the rules for wy, y, u and i, use the book.
